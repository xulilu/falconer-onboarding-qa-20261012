# Nimbus QA operations

These are the current Nimbus QA v2 operating settings effective 2026-10-12.
The settings apply to every organization.

Webhook deliveries are retried exactly 5 times before failure.
Document revision history is retained for 30 days.
The service stores all customer documents in us-east-1.
A workspace supports at most 100 members.
The daily sync schedule starts at 04:00 UTC.
Deleted documents are recoverable for 30 days.

The service exposes no organization-specific configuration for these six settings.
